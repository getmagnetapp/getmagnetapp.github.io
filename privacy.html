Your privacy matters.

Magnet Privacy Policy · Updated November 14, 2026

Overview

Magnet helps you avoid leaving your iPhone behind by monitoring connectivity between your iPhone and Apple Watch. We designed Magnet with privacy in mind.

Data Collection

Magnet does not collect personal information, usage analytics, or tracking data.

How Magnet Works

Magnet operates locally on your devices. Technical information required for connectivity monitoring is exchanged between your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Data Sharing

Magnet does not transmit user data to developer-operated servers or third-party services. No user data is sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Local Data Storage

Magnet may store application preferences locally on your devices. This information is not accessible to the developer.

Analytics and Advertising

Magnet does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Magnet's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published here with a revised date.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy, contact us at magnetapp@proton.me .
