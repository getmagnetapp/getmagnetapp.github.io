Getting started.

  1. Install Magnet on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  2. Open Magnet on both devices.
  3. Enable protection with a single tap.
  4. Wear your Apple Watch to receive alerts.

Frequently asked questions.

How does Magnet work?

Magnet monitors the connection between your iPhone and Apple Watch. When the connection is lost, your watch can alert you.

Does Magnet require an Apple Watch?

Yes. Magnet is designed for an iPhone paired with an Apple Watch.

Can I pause protection?

Yes. You can turn protection off or snooze an alert when needed.

Why didn't I receive an alert immediately?

Alert timing can vary depending on Bluetooth connectivity, device state, and iOS or watchOS background activity.

Does Magnet collect my personal data?

No. Magnet works locally between your devices and does not collect personal information or usage analytics. Read our Privacy Policy.
